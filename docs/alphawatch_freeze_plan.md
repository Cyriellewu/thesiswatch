# AlphaWatch Freeze Plan

## Goal

AlphaWatch is not a project for endlessly redesigning dashboards. Its job is to help you:

- notice major moves in your holdings,
- compare two virtual strategies,
- find non-tech opportunities,
- get a small daily market radar.

The app is useful when it reduces checking and hesitation. It is failing when it becomes another thing to keep redesigning.

## Deadline

**Until 2026-05-15:** only finish the current news-tab consolidation.

Allowed work:

- P0: reduce pending news tags and remove obvious `AI analysis pending` noise.
- P1: make the core story format concrete: what happened, why it matters, impact on your money, action.
- P2: add the holdings story cards.
- P3: make macro items explain portfolio impact.
- P4: keep the advanced workbench folded by default.
- P5: remove empty placeholder sections like "no urgent alerts".

Not allowed before 2026-05-15:

- adding a new major tab,
- adding new data vendors unless a current source is broken,
- redesigning the whole visual system,
- changing the virtual-account premise again,
- building "one more" complex scoring model.

## 30-Day Use Period

**From 2026-05-15 through 2026-06-15:** stop major changes and use AlphaWatch daily.

Allowed during this period:

- bug fixes that unblock daily use,
- false-news fixes,
- broken API/key fixes,
- small copy changes under 20 minutes,
- adding a note to the backlog.

Not allowed during this period:

- new layouts,
- new scoring systems,
- new agent personalities,
- new major workflows,
- rewrites of the news tab.

## Daily Use Checklist

Spend at most 5 minutes:

1. Read the core story.
2. Check holdings story cards.
3. Read today's action.
4. If action says "do nothing", close the app.

Do not open the advanced workbench unless something looks broken.

## Backlog Rule

When you feel the urge to change something, write one line:

```text
YYYY-MM-DD | friction | what annoyed me | did it affect a real decision? yes/no
```

Only items that affect a real decision at least twice during the 30-day use period are eligible for the next build cycle.

## Next Review

On **2026-06-15**, review:

- Which sections you used every day.
- Which sections you never opened.
- Whether virtual Takeover beat your actual portfolio.
- Whether Fresh $5k became more balanced than your real portfolio.
- Whether the news radar caused fewer false alarms.
- Whether you made fewer impulsive trades.

Only then decide the next round of changes.
